# Historical observation audit and target behaviors

This is a mechanism audit and a set of future test targets for the fictional Commonwealth simulator, not a historical reconstruction or an economic forecast. A country or policy label does not map to one control in the game. Each comparison names a time window, observations, an operational model setup, and mechanisms to build. **A historically motivated scenario is allowed to fail while the relevant micro rules are missing.** Do not weaken its expected outcome merely to make the current model pass.

The reproducible probes use two seeded 12×12 worlds (`alder-42`, `marlow`), monthly steps, and no discrete events. They compare trajectories from the same initial state. The assertions live in [historical-patterns.test.ts](../tests/historical-patterns.test.ts), [extreme-scenarios.test.ts](../tests/extreme-scenarios.test.ts), and [ripples.test.ts](../tests/ripples.test.ts). Run them with `npm test`.

## Late Soviet economy, around 1990–91: one administered-price mechanism

A contemporary [World Bank study of the Soviet economy](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/379991468134386346/pdf/777730v30Box370f0the0soviet0economy.pdf) describes widespread shortages, administered prices, state orders, and a monetary overhang. A [World Bank account of the transition](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/309471468763166123/pdf/multi0page.pdf) also describes fiscal deficits financed by monetary emission, disruption of interenterprise links, and output collapse. These were interacting institutions and shocks, not one tax or spending level.

Commonwealth can reproduce a narrower administered-price mechanism. Subsidizing nonfarm sectors pulls labor from farming. With flexible prices, scarcity raises local farm returns and workers gradually return. Under the new food price control, the posted price remains at or below ₡1 while the local scarcity-price signal rises; farms attract fewer workers, and food needs remain unmet longer. The [matched scenario test](../tests/historical-patterns.test.ts) asserts the price gap, local shortage, lower farm share, national shortage, and deaths. This is not a Soviet reconstruction: Commonwealth still has no public ownership, production quotas, ration queues, monetary emission, or interenterprise delivery network.

**Target behavior:** with an administered food price that binds in food-scarce mapxels, residents repeatedly need more food than local stocks and neighboring trade can deliver; unmet demand persists, while a comparable flexible-price scenario pulls workers toward farming and recovers. The matched test checks local unmet needs before the national shortage rate. Explicit shop requests, queues, and rationing remain open.

The current local rule distinguishes the posted consumer price from an unconstrained scarcity signal. `foodSecurity` measures delivered food relative to each mapxel's resident need. The remaining institutional mechanics are explicit shop queues, supplier procurement rules, and material delivery links. Government monetary financing can enter through a shared account, but households and producers must encounter its effects through their local cash, prices, and stocks.

## Argentina's 2024 stabilization: fiscal target only partly met

The [IMF's January 2025 account](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2025/01/17/tr011725-january-2025-world-economic-outlook-update) identifies a large 2024 fiscal contraction, a contraction in activity, and a later rebound. Argentina's statistical agency reported monthly national CPI inflation of [25.5% in December 2023](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/ipc_01_24DBD5D8158C.pdf) and [2.7% in December 2024](https://www.indec.gob.ar/uploads/informesdeprensa/ipc_01_2517A7124C09.pdf). Its household surveys report poverty in the 31 covered urban areas rising to [52.9% in the first half of 2024](https://biblioteca.indec.gob.ar/bases/minde/eph_pobreza_09_24.pdf), then falling to [38.1% in the second half](https://www.indec.gob.ar/ftp/cuadros/publicaciones/indecinforma/indec_informa_04_25.pdf). The [IMF's 2024 program description](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/cr/issues/2024/06/17/argentina-eighth-review-under-the-extended-arrangement-under-the-extended-fund-facility-550548) also names monetary financing, relative-price corrections, reserves, exchange restrictions, and social assistance. These observations have different definitions and cannot be mapped to one Commonwealth indicator.

The model probe first runs 12 months with all seven service promises at their maximum, exhausting budget capacity. It then compares 24 months of unchanged promises with a cut to ₡0.10 per service per resident. The cut restores full funding of the smaller promise, retains an operating reserve, and repays part of the inherited debt through explicit cash transfers; health, safety, and output are worse than in the matched continuation. That is a **fiscal consolidation pattern**, not an Argentina reproduction. The test does not assert that contraction causes later growth. The model has no general CPI, currency, exchange rate, monetary financing, real wages, household poverty rate, or foreign reserve dynamics; food `price` is a local staple price, and `output` is an abstract activity measure rather than GDP.

**Target behavior:** from an inherited fiscal and monetary crisis, tighter public spending and an end to deficit monetization should improve the cash budget and slow the growth of the price basket. A matched trajectory may show an initial fall in real household consumption and activity, followed by recovery as local real purchasing power and production respond. The target should measure both the hardship and recovery phases. This is a hypothesis for the simulator, not a claim that one policy alone caused Argentina's observed path.

To test it, mapxels need a household price basket, real income or purchasing-power measure, and distribution of household resources from which a poverty share can be calculated. Local producers need input costs, prices, and employment responses. Fiscal flows can aggregate through the treasury; debt repayment now has explicit accounting, while monetary issue does not exist. National CPI, poverty, and activity must then be calculated from those local observations. A change to one national `inflation` or `GDP` field would bypass the intended mechanism.

The probe also exposed a concrete action bug: a treasury balance a tiny amount below zero from floating-point settlement blocked a cash-free policy edit. [The action boundary](../src/sim/policy.ts) now rejects unaffordable investments while allowing cash-free policy changes in that state, with a regression in [engine.test.ts](../tests/engine.test.ts).

One calibration question remains open. Cutting an already rationed service promise can raise approval immediately because the `funding` fraction rises, even when realized spending and later health fall. That follows the current approval equation, but it risks rewarding a change in the promise denominator rather than better service. A future test should define whether citizens respond to delivered services, broken promises, or both before the equation is changed.

## US acid-rain controls: directional pollution-health pattern

The [US Environmental Protection Agency's Acid Rain Program account](https://www.epa.gov/acidrain/acid-rain-program) describes a sulfur-dioxide cap-and-trade system. Its [results summary](https://www.epa.gov/acidrain/acid-rain-program-results) reports large emissions reductions and environmental and health benefits. Commonwealth cannot reproduce allowance trading or measured SO₂ exposure. It can test a narrower causal pattern: after 12 months of manufacturing support, enacting Clean Air reduces modeled pollution by more than 0.2, improves health after 24 months, and lowers output relative to an otherwise identical industrial world. The [historical-pattern test](../tests/historical-patterns.test.ts) checks the national trajectory; a [rule test](../tests/rules.test.ts) checks that production in one mapxel raises pollution in a neighbor and Clean Air improves that neighbor's later health. A future institutional target would track actual emissions, compliance, and exposure instead of treating manufacturing share as their proxy.

## US Dust Bowl: persistent-shock target not yet met

A [US Forest Service synthesis](https://www.fs.usda.gov/research/publications/gtr/gtr_wo93b.pdf) describes repeated 1930s drought, soil erosion, lost production, and socioeconomic hardship. Commonwealth's drought event destroys 35% of a region's stored food and now leaves local water stress that cuts subsequent farm production. It does not accumulate erosion or model a sustained multi-year drought. [The event ripple test](../tests/ripples.test.ts) checks the next-month food and price response.

**Target behavior:** repeated drought and poor land management lower soil condition and food production in affected mapxels over several years, create sustained local hardship, and drive migration toward safer communities. Soil condition and movement should be observed locally; national food and population changes are aggregates. The [drought tests](../tests/drought.test.ts) check the first event's next harvest and a sustained exogenous dry period: local harvest, food access, and health fall, while residents leave the affected region. Persistent soil erosion and poor land-management choices remain open; water stress alone does not reproduce the Dust Bowl.

## Micro-first test contract

1. Define the intervention, starting conditions, and monthly changes for mapxels. A national policy can set constraints or move shared funds; production, consumption, prices, health, and migration respond through mapxel rules.
2. Assert the local chain first: what a household requests and receives, what a producer makes and can sell, which cell gains or loses people or resources. Assert conservation and boundedness at each phase.
3. Derive the national outcome from those cells and their trades. Do not add a direct national GDP, inflation, poverty, famine, or approval adjustment merely to hit a historical curve.
4. Compare a matched counterfactual with the same seed and starting map. Include opposing or missing interventions so a target does not pass because of an unrelated rule.
5. Keep a well-defined historical target red when it fails. If the required action or observation does not exist yet, specify its local contract here, then add the executable red test when the representation is introduced. Never substitute food-price movement for general inflation or a population mean of wealth for household poverty.

Historical sources identify patterns and plausible mechanisms; they do not uniquely identify causes. Record each observation's source, time window, unit, scope, and competing causes. A failing target can reveal missing local mechanics, poor calibration, or an incorrect expectation. Diagnose which one before changing the rule or the assertion.
