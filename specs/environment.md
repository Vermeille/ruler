# Environment and local conditions

[I] ENVIRONMENT-HEALTH1: funded health services improve local health access, while pollution and food insecurity worsen it; wealthier residents add a small private health advantage.

[I] ENVIRONMENT-EDUCATION1: funded education services improve local education access, with an additional advantage in wealthier communities.

[I] ENVIRONMENT-INFRA1: funded infrastructure spending improves local infrastructure, and available materials per resident provide an additional boost.

[I] ENVIRONMENT-POLLUTION1: manufacturing and population density create pollution pressure, neighboring industry contributes to it, environmental spending reduces it, and clean-air rules reduce manufacturing pollution pressure.

[I] ENVIRONMENT-SPORTS1: culture spending and a larger sports workforce increase local sports interest.
