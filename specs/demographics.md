# Demographics

[I] DEMOGRAPHICS-AGING1: every population group ages by one month on each simulation step.

[I] DEMOGRAPHICS-LIFESTAGE1: children become adults at eighteen and adults become seniors at sixty-five; entering adulthood or retirement clears occupation and employment status.

[I] DEMOGRAPHICS-BIRTHRATE1: births are resolved annually and become more numerous with better local wellbeing and health, more reproductive adults, and stronger family orientation among those adults.

[I] DEMOGRAPHICS-INHERITANCE1: newborn cohorts usually inherit a parent's archetype, sometimes vary to a nearby archetype, and begin with the parent's health, wellbeing, approval and attitudes plus a small share of parental wealth.

[I] DEMOGRAPHICS-MORTALITY1: ordinary mortality rises when health is worse; seniors become increasingly vulnerable with age and children have higher baseline mortality than adults.

[I] DEMOGRAPHICS-STARVATION1: food insecurity creates additional mortality that becomes sharply worse under severe deprivation, with age and poor health increasing vulnerability and children receiving extra food-deprivation risk.

[I] DEMOGRAPHICS-STARVATIONREPORT1: the mapxel starvation-deaths field reports the severe food-deprivation component for UI and history rather than representing a separate source of deaths.
