# Crime

[I] CRIME-POVERTY1: a larger share of residents living with low household reserves raises local crime pressure.

[I] CRIME-UNEMPLOYMENT1: higher working-age unemployment raises local crime pressure.

[I] CRIME-INEQUALITY1: a place that is poorer than its neighboring land mapxels experiences additional crime pressure from nearby inequality.

[I] CRIME-POLICE1: funded police spending lowers local crime pressure.

[I] CRIME-WELFARE1: funded welfare spending lowers local crime pressure.

[I] CRIME-ADJUST1: observed local crime changes gradually toward the pressure created by resident circumstances and policy rather than jumping instantly to it.
