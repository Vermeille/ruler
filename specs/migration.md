# Migration

[I] MIGRATION-NEEDS1: adults compare nearby places through the needs of their archetype, including food, income, employment, health, safety, housing, education, environment, and culture.

[I] MIGRATION-MOOD1: a place is more attractive to a group when the local community's wellbeing is higher.

[I] MIGRATION-LOCAL1: migration considers only neighboring land mapxels and moves toward the most attractive one when it is better than home.

[I] MIGRATION-MEANS1: residents with fewer reserves are less able to move, even when another place is more attractive.

[I] MIGRATION-ROOTS1: mobility increases a group's propensity to move while community attachment reduces it.

[I] MIGRATION-FREEDOM1: restricting free movement sharply reduces migration rather than disabling it completely.

[I] MIGRATION-CASH1: moving residents carry a proportional share of their origin mapxel's pooled private cash to their destination.
