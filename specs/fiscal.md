# Fiscal state

[I] FISCAL-TAX1: monthly tax collection combines income and business tax rates against local output, but a mapxel cannot pay more tax than the private cash it actually has.

[I] FISCAL-INTEREST1: public debt accrues interest at 0.3% per month.

[I] FISCAL-BORROW1: the government borrows only to cover the forecast service-and-interest cash shortfall, subject to available external credit and a debt ceiling of thirty crowns per resident.

[I] FISCAL-FUNDING1: after paying interest, public services and subsidies are funded proportionally when the treasury cannot afford all promised spending.

[I] FISCAL-SPENDING1: funded subsidies enter local private cash; seventy-two percent of ordinary public-service spending also enters local private cash while the rest leaves to the external economy.

[I] FISCAL-REPAY1: treasury cash left beyond a reserve of six crowns per resident is used to repay outstanding debt principal.
