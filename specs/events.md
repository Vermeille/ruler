# Events

[I] EVENT-WATER-RECOVERY1: existing water stress recovers gradually each month when no new drought pressure replaces it.

[I] EVENT-CRIME1: after a cooldown, a randomly selected place can suffer a violent crime event, with the probability rising strongly with that place's crime level.

[I] EVENT-CRIME-WELLBEING1: a violent crime causes a substantial wellbeing shock where it happens and a smaller wellbeing shock to residents elsewhere in the country.

[I] EVENT-FESTIVAL1: after a cooldown, a randomly selected place can host a local sports festival, with higher sports interest making the event more likely.

[I] EVENT-FESTIVAL-EFFECT1: a sports festival raises local sports interest and wellbeing and brings outside cash into the host mapxel according to its population.

[I] EVENT-DROUGHT1: after a longer cooldown, a drought can randomly strike one region of the country.

[I] EVENT-DROUGHT-EFFECT1: drought destroys part of every affected mapxel's stored food and raises water stress across the affected region.
