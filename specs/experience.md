# Lived experience and attitudes

[I] EXPERIENCE-WELLBEING1: each group's wellbeing combines food, purchasing power, employment, health, safety, housing, education, environment, and culture according to that archetype's needs.

[I] EXPERIENCE-WEALTHBUFFER1: household reserves cushion the wellbeing damage from expensive or insufficient food and contribute to purchasing power.

[I] EXPERIENCE-INCOME1: employed adults' income moves toward local output per resident with an education advantage, while people without employment move toward zero income.

[I] EXPERIENCE-WEALTH1: household wealth changes according to income after necessities and drifts slightly toward the local private-cash level.

[I] EXPERIENCE-HEALTH1: people's health slowly follows the health conditions available where they live.

[I] EXPERIENCE-EDUCATION1: children and adults learn toward local education access, modified by their archetype's education affinity; children learn faster and seniors do not gain education this way.

[I] EXPERIENCE-APPROVAL1: political approval responds to wellbeing, changes in wellbeing, funded public services, freedom-of-assembly alignment, and income tax pressure weighted by materialism.

[I] EXPERIENCE-ENVIRONMENTALISM1: living with more pollution slowly raises environmentalist attitudes above an archetype's baseline.

[I] EXPERIENCE-LIBERTY1: restricting public assembly slowly raises civic-liberty attitudes above an archetype's baseline and reduces approval more among people who already value civic liberty.

[I] ATTITUDE-SOLIDARITY1: lower wellbeing slowly raises solidarity above an archetype's baseline.

[I] ATTITUDE-TRADITIONALISM1: lower wellbeing slowly raises traditionalism above an archetype's baseline.
